Privacy Policy

calendar-widget is a personal desktop app used only by its author. It requests read-only access to Google Calendar to display upcoming events on the Windows taskbar.

Calendar data is read directly from Google on the user's own computer and is never sent to, stored on, or shared with any server or third party. The OAuth token is stored only locally on that computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
